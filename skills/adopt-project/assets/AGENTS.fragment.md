## Beligent SDD

Read `.beligent/config.json` for this project's profile, technologies, artifact
locations, and ticket provider. Read the configured overview and applicable
project conventions before drafting or changing work. Project configuration and
artifacts are project-owned; installed skills are reusable framework resources.

Use `define-work` for requirements, specifications, epics, or ticket management;
`plan-work` for technical planning; `implement-ticket` for approved implementation;
and `verify-work` for acceptance evidence. Reuse canonical artifacts and preserve
their IDs and links. Preliminary tickets may precede architecture; coding requires
ready work and current human authorization for its concrete scope.

Honor the configured approval gates. Record real human decisions and their scope;
do not treat a draft, silence, or passing tests as approval. If approved behavior
or scope changes, return for the affected decision. Report unverified criteria
and unresolved conflicts explicitly.
